Overview

The MoodMachine is the apartment's emotional nervous system. It continuously aggregates environmental readings — time of day, weather, plant health, air quality, and optional perfume — into a single normalized 0–1 mood value. That value is evaluated against per-profile curves to drive every atmospheric output in the scene: light color and intensity, fog density, rain particle emission, audio mix levels, screen color filter, and post-process parameters.

The system is designed to feel organic: mood shifts gradually under most circumstances, ensuring atmosphere reads as a living ambience rather than a switched state.

Player Fantasy

The player should feel that the apartment is breathing. Watering a struggling plant, watching rain begin outside, or spraying a particular perfume should each register as a perceptible, cohesive shift in the world — not as isolated one-off effects. The ideal experience is the player pausing and noticing "it feels different in here now" without being able to name exactly what changed.

Mood is the silent collaborator in every scene.

Source Registration

Any system may register itself as a mood source by providing a string key and a float value in [0, 1]. The MoodMachine maintains a dictionary of all active sources. Sources may update their value at any time or be removed (e.g., perfume wearing off, a plant dying).

Mood Smoothing

Current mood uses MoveTowards — linear rate of change, no overshoot, full traverse in ~2 seconds:

currentMood = MoveTowards(currentMood, target, 0.5 × deltaTime)

This means a storm rolling in doesn't snap the scene to maximum fog — it ramps up over 2 seconds, creating a sense of atmosphere building rather than switching.

Profile System & Perfume Blending

The MoodMachine always has one base profile active. An override profile may be blended in additively on top. Each profile is a MoodMachineProfile ScriptableObject with curves mapping mood [0, 1] to output driver values.

Perfume does not register as a source. Instead it blends in a separate override profile at blendCurrent influence.

Edge Cases

No active sources

Target mood is 0. If this is undesirable at game start, a default ambient source should be registered before play begins. The system does not self-initialize a fallback.

Plant count drops to zero mid-session

LivingFlowerPlantManager should either remove the "LivingPlants" source from the registry or submit 0. If the source remains at 0, it still pulls mood down. If removed, it no longer influences. The implemented behavior is to be confirmed against the actual code.

Perfume change mid-blend

If the player switches perfume type while a previous blend is in progress, blendTarget resets to 0 and begins climbing again for the new profile. The previous override profile fades out as blendCurrent descends. There is no simultaneous cross-fade between two override profiles.

Three sprays overshoot to 1.002

blendTarget is clamped to 1.0 before smoothing. No visual artifact from the overshoot. Three sprays effectively saturates the blend to 100%.

Mood stuck near target (snap behavior)

MoveTowards will not overshoot. If currentMood is within 0.5 × deltaTime of target, it snaps exactly to target. This is visually imperceptible (less than one step per frame).

Weather change during stormy rain

Weather mood jumps to 0.9 immediately as a source value update. The 2-second smoothing on currentMood prevents visual outputs from snapping — rain emission and fog ramp up over ~2 seconds.

Override profile with no base profile assigned

Behavior is undefined in the design. Implementation should guard against null profile and log a warning.

Acceptance Criteria

  • ✓Source averaging: register three sources with values 0.2, 0.6, 0.8. Computed target equals 0.533 (±0.001).
  • ✓Smoothing rate: with currentMood=0 and target=1, after exactly 2 seconds of simulated deltaTime steps, currentMood equals 1.0.
  • ✓TimeOfDay mapping: at game clock 8:00, TimeOfDay source registers 0.0 (±0.01). At 0:00, it registers 0.9 (±0.01).
  • ✓Weather mapping: setting WeatherSystem to Stormy causes Weather source to read 0.9 within one frame.
  • ✓LivingPlants averaging: two plants at health 0.4 and 0.8, LivingPlants source reads 0.6 (±0.001).
  • ✓AirQuality stress penalty: one healthy plant (health=1.0) and one stressed plant (health=1.0) produce AirQuality = 0.65.
  • ✓Perfume single spray: one spray sets blendTarget to 0.334. After 1.25 seconds, blendCurrent reaches 0.334 (within 0.01).
  • ✓Perfume three sprays: three sprays set blendTarget to 1.0 (clamped). After 1.25 seconds, blendCurrent reaches 1.0 (within 0.01).
  • ✓Perfume type change resets blend: spray perfume A (blendCurrent > 0), switch to perfume B. blendTarget resets to 0 and override profile changes.
  • ✓Fog output: at currentMood=1.0, RenderSettings.fogDensity equals 0.03 (±0.001). At currentMood=0.0, equals 0.001.
  • ✓Rain output: at currentMood=1.0, rain particle emission rate is 200/sec. At currentMood=0.0, emission rate is 0.
  • ✓Weather audio threshold: at currentMood=0.39, weather AudioMixer volume is 0 or effectively silent. At currentMood=0.41, volume is non-zero.
  • ✓No sources registered: with zero sources, target=0 and currentMood moves toward 0.
  • ✓Atmosphere at peak mood: at currentMood=1.0, AtmosphereController receives bloom=0.25, grain=2.5, exposure=-0.4, vignette=+0.15.
