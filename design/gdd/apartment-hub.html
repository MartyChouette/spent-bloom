Overview

The Apartment Hub is the persistent home base of Iris. The player browses a small apartment across three distinct physical areas — Kitchen, Living Room, and Entrance — using a Cinemachine spline-dolly camera mounted on a 4-knot closed loop. The camera system supports panning, zooming, edge-scrolling, and a top-down overview mode, with a parallax layer that adds depth to the 2.5D diorama presentation. During date phases, the camera transitions to director-authored preset positions and restricts free movement. All areas are always available with no gating.

Player Fantasy

The player should feel like a quiet observer of their own life — drifting unhurriedly through a small, lived-in space. Browsing the apartment should feel like panning a camera across a handcrafted scene: deliberate, calm, and exploratory. No area should feel inaccessible or locked away. On dates, the camera positions should feel curated, as though the apartment is staging itself to be seen through someone else's eyes.

Camera Architecture

The browse camera runs on a CinemachineSplineDolly mounted on a 4-knot closed spline loop that spans the apartment. The Cinemachine Brain handles blending between the dolly camera and date preset virtual cameras based on priority.

  • Default projection: Orthographic
  • Date preset cameras: May switch to perspective as authored
  • Blend authority: CinemachineBrain — presets must outrank the dolly

Date Phase Overrides

When a date phase begins, the director-authored preset virtual camera takes priority via the Cinemachine Brain. Free movement is restricted but not eliminated:

  • Pan speed reduction: 0.35x of base pan speed (3.0 × 0.35 = 1.05 units/sec effective)
  • Parallax: Continues unless ReduceMotion is set
  • Pan offset accumulation: If player pans away from preset framing, offset accumulates and restores on date end

Area Cycling Disabled

A/D key handlers exist in code for cycling between areas. The UI is currently hidden and the mechanic is not exposed to players. Cycling would wrap from the last area back to the first.

Camera Transitions

When the camera moves between positions (area changes, returning from a preset), it lerps at speed 5. The transition is considered complete when:

done = (cameraPosition - targetPosition).sqrMagnitude < 0.0001

A camera 0.009 units from its target has sqrMagnitude ~0.000081 — below threshold, transition stops.

Edge Cases

ReduceMotion active

Parallax offset and rotation are forced to zero each frame. The parallax component remains in the scene but produces no visual movement. Pan and zoom are unaffected.

InvertScroll active

The scroll delta sign is flipped before zoom step selection. Scrolling up zooms out instead of in. No other input is affected.

Date preset active while player pans

Panning is not fully locked — it is slowed to 0.35x. If the player pans away from the preset framing, the pan offset accumulates. Returning to free-browse mode restores full pan speed.

Top-down mode with date preset

If a date preset activates while top-down is engaged, the Cinemachine Brain blend takes priority and the top-down virtual camera loses priority. On date end, top-down state is not automatically restored — the player must re-press Tab.

Circular clamp at area boundary

If the player pans to exactly the 3-unit radius, position is clamped to the circle's perimeter. No bounce or spring is applied — clamping is hard.

MMB drag while edge-scrolling

Both inputs contribute simultaneously. The resulting velocity is the sum of the drag delta (scaled by 0.01) and the edge-scroll speed. This can cause faster-than-expected movement near corners.

Zoom at minimum or maximum step

Scroll input beyond the bounds is ignored. No animation or feedback plays at the limit. The zoom stays at step 1 (ortho size 100) or step 5 (ortho size 30) and further scroll does nothing.

Area cycling (disabled)

The A/D key handlers exist in code but the UI is hidden. If the UI is re-enabled, cycling wraps from the last area back to the first.

Acceptance Criteria

  • ✓Pan bounds: Place camera at max circular clamp radius (3 units). Verify it cannot be moved further outward by any pan method. Position must not exceed 3.0001 units from area center.
  • ✓Zoom steps: Starting at step 1 (ortho size 100), scroll down five times and verify ortho size reaches 30. Scroll once more and verify ortho size remains 30.
  • ✓InvertScroll: Enable InvertScroll in accessibility settings. Scrolling up must zoom out (size increases). Scrolling down must zoom in (size decreases).
  • ✓ReduceMotion: Enable ReduceMotion. Move mouse to screen corner. Verify no parallax offset or rotation is applied to any parallax layer.
  • ✓Edge-scroll activation delay: Move cursor within 60px of screen edge. Camera must not move for the first 0.1 seconds. After 0.1s it must begin panning toward the edge.
  • ✓Top-down toggle: Press Tab. Verify camera lerps to height 12, ortho size 8. Press Tab again. Verify camera returns to previous browse position.
  • ✓Date pan speed reduction: Trigger a date phase with preset camera active. Verify WASD panning is perceptibly slower (measured speed at or below 1.05 units/sec = 3.0 × 0.35).
  • ✓Camera transition completion: Initiate a position transition. Verify camera stops updating once sqrMagnitude to target is below 0.0001.
  • ✓Area gating: Verify at game start that Kitchen, Living Room, and Entrance are all accessible and interactable without any unlock condition.
  • ✓MMB drag: Click and drag middle mouse button 100 pixels. Verify camera translates 1.0 world units (100px × 0.01 multiplier).
