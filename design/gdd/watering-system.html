Overview

The Watering System lets the player use a physical watering can to tend their potted plants. Picking up the can and moving it near a plant triggers a magnetic snap; tilting to pour fills a 2D cross-section UI representing the pot's interior. Water level is governed by pot geometry — different pot shapes require different pour rates to rise at a consistent visual speed.

The target fill sits at 70% of pot height, with an 8-point tolerance window on either side. Overflow spills onto the floor. Water level persists between sessions and dries overnight at a rate scaled by the next day's weather. Chronic underwatering causes leaf shedding; chronic overwatering is marked as a future consequence TODO.

Player Fantasy

The player should feel the gentle satisfaction of a daily ritual done right: lifting a familiar object, tilting it with deliberate care, watching the water rise to exactly the right line. Getting it perfect should feel effortless once learned but never automatic — the oscillating pour rate and varying pot shapes mean the player must stay present.

Forgetting to water should carry a quiet cost, not a catastrophic penalty: leaves fall, the plant droops, a small mess appears. The system rewards attentiveness and forgives distraction.

Interaction Flow

  1. Player grabs the watering can via ObjectGrabber.
  2. When the can comes within 1.5 meters of a plant's snap point, ObjectGrabber detects proximity and highlights the interaction.
  3. Clicking while in snap range snaps the can to the pouring position and begins WateringManager state transition: Idle → Pouring.
  4. Player holds interaction input to continue pouring; releasing transitions to Done then back to Idle after a settle period.

Pour Rate Formula

pourRate = 0.1 × shapeFillMultiplier × oscillation oscillation = 1 + 0.15 × sin(2π × 2 × t) // frequency = 2 Hz, amplitude = ±15% of base, t = seconds since pour began
VariableDescription
0.1Base rate constant (water level units per second)
shapeFillMultiplierGeometry correction for current pot shape and fill height
oscillationSinusoidal variance; keeps pouring tactile and non-mechanical

Example — TallCylinder at t = 0.25s (oscillation peak)

oscillation = 1 + 0.15 × sin(π/2) = 1.15 pourRate = 0.1 × 1.0 × 1.15 = 0.115 units/second

Example — RoundBulb at h = 0.0 (narrow bottom)

w(0.0) = 0.5 + 0.5 × sin(0) = 0.5 shapeFillMultiplier = 1 / max(0.5, 0.3) = 2.0 pourRate = 0.1 × 2.0 × oscillation // doubles at the base

Foam Dynamics

// Foam level foamLevel = waterLevel + max(foamAccumulation, 0.03) // Foam generation (while pouring) foamAccumulation rate = pourRate × 2.5 // Foam decay (while not pouring) foamDecay rate = -0.2 × deltaTime

The minimum foam offset of 0.03 ensures foam is always visually rendered above the water surface.

Overnight Drying

newWaterLevel = waterLevel - (baseDryingRate × weatherMultiplier)

baseDryingRate is defined in PlantDefinition per plant species.

Water Target and Tolerance

targetMin = idealWaterLevel - tolerance = 0.7 - 0.08 = 0.62 targetMax = idealWaterLevel + tolerance = 0.7 + 0.08 = 0.78 "Looks perfect!" when: targetMin <= waterLevel <= targetMax

Consequences of Poor Watering

Underwatering

50% chance per morning that the plant sheds a leaf, spawning a trash object near the plant's base. This registers with TidyScorer as a mess that must be cleaned before a date.

Leaf shed check runs at day start. A plant restored from a previous session at an underwatered level will roll the check on the first morning.

Overwatering TODO

Visual water spill stain at pot base. A SpillSurface quad is spawned at ground level adjacent to the pot when waterLevel exceeds 1.0.

Full consequence implementation is marked TODO. SpillSurface spawns immediately on overflow; waterLevel is clamped at 1.1 and foamLevel at 1.2.

Soil Visual Feedback

Soil color linearly interpolates between dry and wet based on current water level:

StateColor (RGB normalized)
Dry (level = 0.0) R: 0.55, G: 0.40, B: 0.25
Wet (level = 1.0) R: 0.30, G: 0.22, B: 0.12

Other Visual Indicators

  • Fill line marker: Fixed at idealWaterLevel (0.7); serves as a static target guide.
  • Water line marker: Moves dynamically to show the current water level.
  • Drain drip: Visible when waterLevel > 0.5, signaling the plant can drain if conditions allow.
  • Foam: Always rendered at least 0.03 above the water surface.

Persistence

  • Water level stored on WaterablePlant, saved to disk.
  • SaveWaterLevel throttled to 4 Hz max to avoid write overhead during continuous pouring.
  • On session load, stored level is restored before overnight drying is applied.

Camera Behavior

ApartmentManager suppresses standard camera pan input while the player is in an active watering interaction. Normal control resumes when the can is released or snapped away from the plant.

Acceptance Criteria

  • ✓Magnetic snap: Moving watering can within 1.5m of any plant triggers snap highlight. Clicking begins pouring. QA: measure distance at highlight activation.
  • ✓Pour rate oscillation: Record waterLevel every 0.1s during 30s pour on TallCylinder. Confirm rate between 0.085 and 0.115 units/second (base ±15%). QA: unit test or scene recording.
  • ✓Target status messages: Pour to 0.62 (min), 0.70 (target), 0.79 (just above max) and stop. Confirm "Looks perfect!" at 0.62 and 0.70, "A bit much..." at 0.79. QA: manual test.
  • ✓Overflow spawns SpillSurface: Pour until waterLevel exceeds 1.0. Confirm SpillSurface quad appears at ground level and waterLevel does not exceed 1.1. QA: scene inspection after overflow.
  • ✓Overnight drying — Clear weather: Plant at 0.7 on Clear night. Confirm next morning = 0.7 - (baseDryingRate × 1.5). QA: set weather Clear, advance day, check WaterablePlant.waterLevel.
  • ✓Overnight drying — Snowy weather: Same plant, Snowy weather. Confirm reduction = baseDryingRate × 0.3. QA: same method, weather = Snowy.
  • ✓Leaf shedding probability: Over 100 simulated mornings with underwatered plant, confirm leaf shed occurs 35–65 times (50% ± noise). QA: automated unit test iterating morning event.
  • ✓Persistence across sessions: Set water level to 0.55, save, restart, confirm restored level is 0.55 before any day-start drying runs. QA: log waterLevel immediately on session load.
  • ✓Save throttle: Pour continuously 2 seconds, force crash at 1-second mark. Confirm restored water level is no more than 0.25s of pour behind crash point. QA: debug-forced crash during pour.
  • ✓Camera suppression: Begin watering interaction; confirm camera pan input is non-responsive. Release can; confirm pan resumes. QA: manual input test during and after interaction.
