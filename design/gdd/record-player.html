Overview

The record player is a fully physical vinyl workflow: the player peeks at an album sleeve, extracts the disc, carries it across the room, places it on the turntable, watches the tone arm swing down, and hears music begin. Each step has tactile feedback through animation and sound.

The system integrates with both the MoodMachine (the playing record's mood value becomes a named source) and the date evaluation system (ReactableTag drives music judgment during date phases). Auto-eject handles the common case of swapping records gracefully.

Player Fantasy

The player should feel the ritual of vinyl. Pulling a record out of its sleeve, walking it over, setting the needle — these gestures are slow, deliberate, and satisfying in proportion to their care. The apartment should feel different depending on what's playing, and the act of choosing a record should feel like choosing a mood.

Pausing music by clicking the turntable, rather than a UI button, reinforces that everything in this space is tangible.

Date Integration

While a record is playing (ReactableTag.IsActive = true):

  • Phase 1 (music judgment): the date NPC evaluates the playing record's genre and mood as part of their first impression.
  • Phase 3 (item evaluation): the record is included as an interactive item subject to date scoring rules.

PEEK — Hovering the cursor over an AlbumSleeve triggers the peek animation: the sleeve tilts (tiltSpeed applied each frame) and the vinyl inside slides upward (peekSpeed applied each frame). A peek SFX plays on hover-enter. The animation reverses on hover-exit. This gives the player a preview that a vinyl disc is present without committing to extraction.

Auto-Eject

When TryAcceptVinyl() is called and a different vinyl is already on the platter:

  1. Current record stops (StopMusic, RemoveSource)
  2. ToneArm snaps to rest (instant — no tween)
  3. Current disc animates back to its matching AlbumSleeve (0.3s slide-in)
  4. New disc is placed on the platter
  5. Lid opens (or stays open if already open)

Same-vinyl placement is a no-op: If the incoming disc matches the one on the platter, nothing happens. No eject animation, no restart.

Platter Rotation Speed

Speed ramps up/down using MoveTowards for a gradual, smooth acceleration without overshooting.

rotationThisFrame = MoveTowards(currentSpeed, targetSpeed, rampRate × deltaTime)
plateTransform.rotation += rotationThisFrame × deltaTime

targetSpeed = 33.3 deg/sec (play) | 0 (pause/stop)
rampRate = 33.3 / spinTransitionDuration = 33.3 / 1.5 = 22.2 deg/sec²
VariableDefaultNotes
targetSpeed33.3 deg/s (playing), 0 (paused)Real vinyl: 33.3 RPM = 33.3 × 360/60 ≈ 200 deg/s — value here is artistic, not physical
spinTransitionDuration1.5sTime to reach full speed from 0
rampRate22.2 deg/sec/secAfter 0.75s of spin-up, currentSpeed ≈ 16.65 deg/s (halfway)

Lid Tween (Smoothstep)

t_raw = elapsedTime / 0.4
t = t_raw × t_raw × (3 − 2 × t_raw)    // smoothstep
lid.localRotation = Lerp(closedAngle, openAngle, t)

closedAngle = (-80, 0, 0) Euler
openAngle = (0, 0, 0) Euler
duration = 0.4 seconds

ToneArm Swing (EaseInOut)

t = EaseInOut(elapsedTime / 1.2)
arm.localRotation = Lerp(restAngle, playAngle, t)

duration = 1.2 seconds
needle-drop SFX fires at t=1.0 (SwingToPlay)
needle-lift SFX fires at t=0.0 (SwingToRest, i.e. at swing start)

Magnetic Snap Radius

snapActive = Vector3.Distance(disc.position, platePlacementPoint.position) < 0.6

Snap radius = 0.6 meters in world space. When active, the disc visually snaps toward the placement point to guide the player.

Edge Cases

Placing same vinyl again

TryAcceptVinyl() is a no-op. No auto-eject, no animation restart, no state change.

Dropping vinyl on the floor

Physics takes over once the grab is released outside the snap radius. The disc becomes a physics prop. The player can pick it up again. No state is corrupted.

Clicking turntable during 0.3s slide-in animation

Play interaction should be blocked until the placement animation completes. Guard with an isAnimating flag.

ToneArm interrupted mid-swing

If the player interacts while the arm is mid-swing, the swing animation should complete or snap to its destination before responding. Interrupting mid-swing risks visual artifacts and audio sync issues.

musicClip and musicClipPath both null

No audio plays. RecordSlot logs a warning. The record still places, the arm still swings, the ReactableTag still activates — only the audio is absent.

AlbumSleeve has no vinyl after extract

The sleeve enters an empty state. Hovering shows no peek animation. Clicking is a no-op.

Returning wrong vinyl to sleeve

The system only accepts the disc whose RecordDefinition matches the sleeve's reference. A mismatch results in no action and potentially a rejection SFX (confirm with implementation).

Auto-eject during date phase

Auto-ejecting a record mid-date removes its MoodMachine source immediately, shifting atmosphere. This is intentional — the player bears responsibility for swapping records during a date.

Acceptance Criteria

  • ✓Peek animation triggers: hover over sleeve. Sleeve tilts, disc slides up. Hover-exit reverses.
  • ✓Extract unparents disc: click sleeved vinyl. VinylDisc is no longer a child of AlbumSleeve and is held by ObjectGrabber.
  • ✓Magnetic snap: carry disc to within 0.6m of turntable. Disc visually snaps toward platter placement point.
  • ✓Placement positions disc correctly: disc appears centered at platePlacementPoint. Not parented to turntable transform.
  • ✓Lid opens on placement: lid completes open tween within 0.4 seconds.
  • ✓ToneArm swing duration: time between click and needle-drop SFX is 1.2 seconds (±0.1).
  • ✓Music plays after arm swing: after needle-drop SFX, music is audible and AudioManager reports IsPlaying=true.
  • ✓MoodMachine source registered: while music plays, MoodMachine has an active "Music" source matching record's moodValue.
  • ✓ReactableTag active while playing: ReactableTag.IsActive is true. After pause, it is false.
  • ✓Pause preserves position: pause at ~30 seconds. Resume. Music continues from ~30 seconds, not beginning.
  • ✓Eject-to-hand stops music: AudioManager.IsPlaying is false. MoodMachine has no "Music" source. Arm snaps to rest instantly (no tween).
  • ✓Return to sleeve: hold extracted disc, click matching sleeve. Disc reparents and 0.3s slide-in plays.
  • ✓Auto-eject on new placement: place Vinyl A (playing), then place Vinyl B. Vinyl A animates back to sleeve. Vinyl B is on platter.
  • ✓Same vinyl re-placement is no-op: place Vinyl A, eject, place again. No auto-eject animation plays.
  • ✓Resources.Load path: assign only musicClipPath (no musicClip). Confirm music plays with no NullReferenceException.
