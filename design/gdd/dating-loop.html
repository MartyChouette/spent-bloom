Overview

The Dating Loop is the primary progression spine of Spent Bloom. Over a 7-day calendar the player receives phone calls from potential dates, arranges visits, and hosts them across three sequential evaluation phases: Entrance (first impressions), Kitchen (drink-making), and Reveal (apartment walkthrough). Each phase feeds affection gains or losses into a running total. A passing grade earns a trimmed flower. Three successful dates with a single character advances their personal arc. Seven distinct characters cycle through the week, with the tutorial date forced on Day 1 and each character withheld from repeat appearance until all others have been seen.

Player Fantasy

The player should feel like someone who genuinely wants to make a good impression — cleaning the apartment not because a prompt says to, but because a real person is coming over. Anticipation builds from the moment the phone rings. Each date should feel like a small social puzzle: the player reads the character's posted ad, infers their preferences, and tries to curate a space that reflects them.

Success should feel warm and earned; failure should feel instructive rather than punitive, leaving the player thinking about what to change before the next visitor arrives.

Calendar & Character Scheduling

  • Game runs on a 7-day cycle managed by GameClock.
  • Each morning, DayManager posts a newspaper personal ad for that day's available character.
  • Day 1 is always the tutorial date; _alwaysPositive = true forces every evaluation to return Like regardless of conditions.
  • No character repeats until all 7 characters have appeared at least once.
  • DateHistory records the outcome of every date (grade, affection, flower awarded, day index).

Phone & Scheduling Flow

  1. PhoneController rings at a scripted or random point during the Morning phase.
  2. Player must physically walk to the phone and answer it.
  3. Answering commits the date; character arrives during Exploration phase.
  4. If the phone is not answered, no date occurs that day.

The Three Date Phases

Phase 1 — Entrance

The date makes 4 simultaneous or near-simultaneous judgments on entry:

  • Music — Evaluated against the character's surface multiplier for the active track. Acts as a multiplier on subsequent phase judgments.
  • Perfume — Intensity [0.34–0.99] = Like  |  Below 0.34 = Neutral  |  Above 0.99 = Dislike. Room smell > 2x perfume threshold downgrades one tier.
  • Cleanliness — Evaluated against TidyScorer output. Threshold is per-character.
  • Outfit — WIP — DISABLED No affection events generated currently.

Phase 2 — Kitchen

Player makes the character a drink. Score range [0, 100].

ConditionOutcome
Liked recipe AND score ≥ 60Like
Score ≥ 80 (any recipe)Like (override)
Disliked recipeDislike (regardless of score)
All other casesNeutral

Phase 3 — Reveal

Character walks the apartment and reacts to items tagged with ReactableTag. Highest total affection potential.

  • Cache building: BuildRevealCache runs during fade-in; 10 items per frame, sorted descending by surface multiplier (3x → 2x → 1x).
  • Presentation: Items surfaced to player staggered 0.6s apart; character evaluates at 2.0s mark of investigation.
  • Player interaction: Player may click any visible item to surface it immediately, bypassing the stagger.
  • Excursions: Character wanders every 4s (90% chance), investigates for 3s, evaluates at 2.0s mark.

Arc Progression

Three successful dates (any grade above F) with the same character completes their personal arc, unlocking arc-specific narrative content stored in DateHistory.

Dormant Systems Dormant

  • Ambient mood drift: Every 15s, affection drifts +0.5 (mood match) or -0.25 (mismatch). Disabled.
  • Mid-date fail thresholds: Affection floors that trigger early date-end. Intentionally disabled; outcome always graded, never hard-failed mid-date.
  • moodMultiplier (1.5x match / 0.5x mismatch): Being replaced by discrete per-phase evaluations. Currently dormant.

Acceptance Criteria

  • ✓Day 1 tutorial lock: All three phase evaluations return Like on Day 1 regardless of apartment state, perfume level, or drink score. QA: set worst-case conditions, confirm grade ≥ A.
  • ✓No character repeats before full rotation: Over 7 consecutive days with phone answered, all 7 characters appear exactly once. QA: record names from newspaper ads across 7 days.
  • ✓Phase sequencing: Phases fire in order Morning → Exploration → DateInProgress → FlowerTrimming (if qualified) → Evening. QA: debug log to each phase transition across 5 dates.
  • ✓Flower threshold: Affection at 30 loads trimming scene. Affection at 29 does not. QA: force via debug menu; observe scene load behavior.
  • ✓Reaction delta correctness: Character with reactionStrength 1.0, Like on 2x item, mood dormant. Confirm affection increases by exactly 10.0. QA: unit test in ReactionEvaluator.
  • ✓Perfume downgrade: Intensity 0.5 (Like), threshold 0.4, roomSmell 0.9 (> 0.8). Confirm result degrades to Neutral. QA: automated test against EvaluatePerfume.
  • ✓Phase 3 cache sort: After BuildRevealCache, items appear to player in descending surface-multiplier order (3x before 2x before 1x). QA: log item order at phase start.
  • ✓Arc completion: After 3 successful dates with the same character, arc content triggers at Evening. QA: run 3 dates, confirm arc flag in DateHistory and content plays.
  • ✓Grade boundaries: Confirm grade assignment at affection values 39, 40, 59, 60, 74, 75, 89, 90. QA: unit test against grading function with those inputs.
  • ✓No phone, no date: Player doesn't approach phone during Morning → Evening loads with no date content and no DateHistory entry for that day. QA: let morning timer expire.
