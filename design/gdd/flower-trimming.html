Overview

Flower Trimming is a focused minigame unlocked at the end of a date when the player earns sufficient affection. The game's main scene loads a dedicated flower-trimming scene additively, offset 50 meters on the Y axis to avoid spatial conflict. The player makes cuts using a plane-based mesh slicer, guided by the ideal parameters defined in IdealFlowerDefinition for the active flower species.

Three scoring components — stem length, cut angle, and part condition — combine into a normalized score that determines how many days the resulting flower will live in the apartment. A perfect cut earns a flower that lasts up to 10 days. Bad scores produce an ugly flower rather than ending the session (soft-fail).

Player Fantasy

The player should feel like a florist with something to prove. The flower arrives from the date with its own imperfections — withered leaves, bent stems — and the player must decide what to keep, what to cut, and where to cut it. The act of cutting should feel precise and slightly nerve-wracking: the blade commits the moment it lands.

A clean cut at the perfect angle and length should feel like a small, private triumph. An imperfect flower that still survives for a few days is a story: a reminder of how the date went, living on the windowsill until it quietly fades.

Session Entry

  • Triggered by DayPhaseManager transitioning to the FlowerTrimming phase.
  • Entry condition: final date affection ≥ 30, OR the date character's guaranteeFlower flag is set.
  • FlowerTrimmingBridge loads the trimming scene additively with a 50-meter Y offset from the main scene origin.
  • FlowerSessionController orchestrates the session from scene load to score submission.

Soft-Fail vs. Hard-Fail

The following conditions previously triggered a hard game-over. Under allowGameOver = false, they now produce a score penalty instead.

  • ✎ Crown removed from stem — component 3 crown score = 0.0 Soft Fail
  • ✎ Stem too high or too low past hardFailDelta — stem score = 0.0 Soft Fail
  • ✎ Cut angle too far from ideal beyond hardFailDelta — angle score = 0.0 Soft Fail
  • ✎ Required special part removed — that part's score = 0.0 Soft Fail

Crown fail Y-failsafe: If the crown is detected as removed but score state has not resolved within 3 seconds, the system forces a timeout and scores the crown as missing.

Game-Over Trigger Checklist

Show mode:

In soft-fail mode, all conditions below produce a 0.0 score on the affected component. The session always completes. allowGameOver = false.

  • 🟦 Crown removed — crown component scores 0.0; flower spawns with heavily penalized score Ugly Flower
  • 🟦 Stem too high — stem beyond idealStemLength + hardFailDelta; stem score = 0.0 Ugly Flower
  • 🟦 Stem too low — stem below idealStemLength - hardFailDelta; stem score = 0.0 Ugly Flower
  • 🟦 Angle too far — cut angle diverges beyond hardFailDelta from 45°; angle score = 0.0 Ugly Flower
  • 🟦 Special part removed — required part with allowedMissing=false cut away; part score = 0.0 Ugly Flower

score_stem = clamp(1 - |currentLen - idealStemLength| / hardFailDelta, 0, 1)

Edge Cases

Crown removed (soft-fail)

Crown removal previously ended the session. Now the crown component scores 0.0 for its part-condition weight. If the crown carries significant scoreWeight, the overall score drops substantially but the session continues.

Crown fail Y-failsafe (3s timeout)

If crown removal is detected but FlowerGameBrain has not resolved the score state after 3 seconds, the system forces a timeout and continues evaluation as if the crown is missing.

Cut grace window (0.15s)

If the player attempts a second cut within 0.15 seconds of the first, the second cut's joint-break events may be suppressed. Re-cuts this close together are effectively treated as a single cut.

All parts missing

If every part is removed and none had allowedMissing = true, all per-part scores are 0.0, Component 3 contributes 0.0, and the normalizedScore reflects only stem and angle components. The flower always spawns with at least 1 day alive.

Ideal stem length of 0 (misconfigured)

If idealStemLength = 0, any non-zero stem length diverges from ideal immediately. The score will be 0.0 for any remaining stem. This is a data configuration error; no runtime fallback.

Scene offset collision (Y > 50m)

The 50-meter Y offset assumes the main scene uses Y values well below 50m. If the main scene extends above Y = 50m (e.g., tall building exteriors), visual bleed between scenes may occur. This is a level design constraint, not handled in code.

Guarantee with poor score

If the date set guaranteeFlower, the flower spawns regardless of score. The guarantee does not override the score-to-days calculation; a guaranteed session with a score of 0.3 still produces a short-lived flower.

Acceptance Criteria

  • ✓Scene loads additively at Y+50: confirm trimming scene root is offset exactly 50m on Y from the main scene origin.
  • ✓Stem score at ideal: cut stem to exactly idealStemLength. Confirm score_stem = 1.0.
  • ✓Stem score at hard fail: cut stem to idealStemLength - hardFailDelta. Confirm score_stem = 0.0.
  • ✓Cut angle score at 45 degrees: make a cut at exactly 45 degrees. Confirm score_angle = 1.0.
  • ✓Part condition — matching ideal: all parts present and fresh. Confirm per-part score = 1.0.
  • ✓Part condition — missing disallowed: required part removed with allowedMissing = false. Confirm per-part score = 0.0.
  • ✓Soft-fail produces flower, not game-over: remove the crown. Confirm session completes, flower spawns, no game-over screen appears.
  • ✓Crown Y-failsafe triggers at 3s: remove crown; block score resolution for 4 seconds. Confirm failsafe fires at the 3-second mark.
  • ✓Score-to-days mapping: score=1.0 → 10 days; score=0.0 → 1 day; score=0.5 → 5 or 6 days.
  • ✓Combined score formula: score_stem=0.9, score_angle=0.8, score_parts=0.85 → normalizedScore=0.855.
  • ✓Cut grace window: query joint break at 0.05s after cut (no event), then at 0.20s (events processed).
  • ✓Guarantee path: set guaranteeFlower = true, give a score of 0.0. Confirm flower still spawns in the apartment.
